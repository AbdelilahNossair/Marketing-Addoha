## Slide 2 — The Problem (Why Now)

**Not a brand problem. A cost-of-living problem.**

| Indicator | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity tariff increase (2024) | **+18%** | ONEE Morocco |
| Households citing bills as top post-purchase worry | **62%** | HCP Survey 2024 |
| Average monthly household energy spend (urban) | **MAD 600** | Industry estimate |
| Addoha gross margin (2024) | **25.4%** | Addoha IFRS FY2024 |

**Insight:** Moroccan middle-income buyers can afford to *buy* a home — but they worry about affording to *live in it*. Energy costs are the invisible second mortgage.

## Slide 3 — The Strategy (One Sentence)

**Add low-cost green features to every unit. Charge a small premium. Let the energy savings sell the product.**

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Green features cost/unit | **MAD 6,000** |
| Price premium/unit | **MAD 7,000** |
| Net margin gain/unit | **MAD 1,000** |
| Customer payback | **~2 years** |
| 10-year customer savings | **~MAD 30,000** |

**Tagline:** *"Tkhallas dar w tn9es l facture"* — Pay your home and reduce your bills.

## Slide 5 — The 4Ps

| P | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Solar water heaters (-40% hot water costs) + thermal insulation (-15–25% cooling/heating) + optimized orientation. No complex tech. Zero structural changes. |
| **Price** | MAD 7,000 premium → 2-year payback → MAD 30,000 savings over 10 years. Sell the investment, not the feature. |
| **Place** | Showrooms with live green mockups, social media ads anchored on MAD saved/month, on-site savings calculator, WhatsApp-first sales. |
| **Promotion** | Economic messaging only. "Your bill drops from day one." Never: eco, sustainability, carbon, luxury, premium. |
