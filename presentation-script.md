# Addoha Green Homes — Presentation Script

> **Total target duration: 2:40–2:50** | Green Homes only | Headline: *A House That Pays You Back*
>
> Word count guidance: ~370–390 words at a natural presentation pace (~145 wpm) hits 2:40–2:50.

---

## [SLIDE 1 — Title] *(5 seconds)*

"Good morning. Our strategy for Addoha is called *A House That Pays You Back*."

---

## [SLIDE 2 — The Problem] *(25 seconds)*

"Let's start with the problem. In 2024, Moroccan electricity tariffs rose 18%. Sixty-two percent of households say energy bills are their number-one concern after buying a home. The average urban family spends 600 dirhams a month on energy alone. So the real question for Addoha's buyers is not 'Can I afford to buy?' — it's 'Can I afford to live in it?'"

---

## [SLIDE 3 — The Strategy] *(20 seconds)*

"Our answer is simple. We add solar water heaters and thermal insulation to every Addoha unit at a construction cost of 6,000 dirhams. We charge a 7,000-dirham premium. That's 1,000 dirhams of additional margin per unit — and the buyer pays back the premium in just two years through lower bills."

---

## [SLIDE 4 — STP] *(15 seconds)*

"We target first-time buyers and young families in the 300,000 to 700,000-dirham band — the segment most sensitive to monthly costs. Our positioning: the most practical way to own a home and reduce your cost of living from day one."

---

## [SLIDE 5 — The 4Ps] *(20 seconds)*

"Product: solar heaters cut hot water costs by 40%, insulation reduces cooling and heating loads by up to 25% — no complex technology, no structural changes. Price: a 7,000-dirham premium that saves 30,000 over ten years. Place: showrooms with live green mockups and savings calculators. Promotion: economic messaging only — 'Your bill drops from day one.' Never sustainability, never eco."

---

## [SLIDE 6 — Company Profitability] *(20 seconds)*

"On Addoha's side, we have two levers. Lever A: every green unit sold nets 1,000 dirhams in margin. At 10,000 units, that's 10 million. Lever B: product differentiation accelerates volume. A 5% lift generates 33 million more in gross profit. Combined base case: 43 million dirhams in additional gross profit."

---

## [SLIDE 7 — Client Profitability] *(15 seconds)*

"On the buyer's side, the math is even clearer. They pay 7,000 once and save roughly 250 dirhams every month. That's 30,000 dirhams over ten years — a 329% return on their premium. And as energy prices keep rising, the savings accelerate."

---

## [SLIDE 8 — Investment & ROI] *(20 seconds)*

"The investment is lean. The 6,000-dirham construction cost is recovered through the premium — it's not a loss, it's COGS. The annual campaign costs 5 to 8 million dirhams — just 0.3% of revenue. Break-even requires only 8,000 units. In the conservative scenario, year-one ROI is 114%. By year two, it reaches 463%."

---

## [SLIDE 9 — Roadmap] *(15 seconds)*

"Execution runs in five phases over 18 months. We start with supplier integration, pilot 500 to 1,000 green units, launch the campaign, validate with real data — and the critical KPI is an 80% premium acceptance rate before we scale."

---

## [SLIDE 10 — Closing] *(15 seconds)*

"To summarize: 6,000 dirhams in, 7,000 out, 1,000 in margin. 250 dirhams saved for every family every month. 43 million in gross profit at scale. And every resident who mentions their lower electricity bill becomes unpaid marketing. *A House That Pays You Back.* Thank you."

---

**Total estimated time: ~2:50**

> Tip: If running over, compress Slide 5 (4Ps) by dropping one P. If running under, pause briefly after the closing line before "Thank you."
